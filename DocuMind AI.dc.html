Section 4 — Leave and Time Off

4.1 Annual leave. Employees are entitled to 20 annual leave days per year, accrued monthly from the start date. Unused days carry over for one quarter into the following year.

4.2 Sick leave. Up to 10 paid sick days are available each year. A medical note is required for absences longer than three consecutive days.

4.3 Personal leave. Two personal days may be taken each year with a manager's approval, and do not carry over.
